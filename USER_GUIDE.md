# FocusBoard: End-User Guide & Feature Walkthrough

## 1. Getting Started
FocusBoard works seamlessly as a desktop web app and as an installable Progressive Web App (PWA) on mobile devices.

### 1.1 Account Creation & Sign In
1. Open the application.
2. Click **Sign In** in the top header.
3. Switch to **Register** if you are a new user. Enter your Name, Email, and Password.
4. Once authenticated, your tasks and workspaces will automatically synchronize with the cloud database.

---

## 2. Task Management & Workflows
- **Create a Task**: Click the `+` button in the header or press `N` on your keyboard.
- **Edit / Update**: Click any task card to open the task editor modal.
- **Change Status**: Move tasks between *Todo*, *In Progress*, and *Completed*.
- **Filter & Search**: Use the search input or composite filter buttons (Status, Priority) on the dashboard to narrow down tasks.

---

## 3. Global Command Palette & Keyboard Shortcuts
- Press `Cmd + K` (Mac) or `Ctrl + K` (Windows) anywhere in the application to open the **Command Palette**.
- Quickly navigate between workspaces, search tasks, toggle AI planning, or export reports.
- Press `?` to open the Keyboard Shortcuts modal.

---

## 4. AI Productivity Planner & Coach
- Navigate to **AI Planner** in the sidebar.
- Enter your available working hours for the day and click **Generate Daily Plan**.
- The planner applies algorithmic knapsack optimization to assign your high-priority tasks into focused time blocks.
- Chat with the **Productivity Coach** to uncover workload bottlenecks and prioritize urgent assignments.

---

## 5. Working Offline
When network connectivity is lost:
- An offline indicator appears: *"Working Offline — Changes will auto-sync when connected"*.
- You can continue creating, editing, and completing tasks.
- When your internet connection is restored, the offline sync engine automatically replays pending actions in the background.
